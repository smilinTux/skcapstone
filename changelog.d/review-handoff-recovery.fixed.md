A finished review can no longer get stuck because its guarded handoff failed. The authority cycle now detects review cards whose reviewer exited 0 and holds a committed REVIEW-DECISION but has no native verdict for its current claim, and asks the reviewer host to re-run exactly the worker's own guarded handoff (one shared recipe in `production_brief.source_review_handoff_recipe`) as a small managed unit, fenced on claim, source head, packet and decision hashes, and idempotent. Results are logged as REVIEW_HANDOFF_RECOVERED or REVIEW_HANDOFF_RECOVERY_REFUSED|reason. If the workspace or packet is gone or changed, the dead reviewer's claim is released through the governed path (never a PASS-holding claim, never a source card) and the review is re-offered. Worker exit evidence now records `handoff-failed` when a review exits 0 without a native verdict.
