The fleet dispatcher now has one cycle deadline (`skcapstone.fleet.cycle_deadline.CycleDeadline`) read by every phase: startup and review sweeps, pool fold, remote review and builder offers, lane selection, launch and review observation stop admitting work at a per-phase share of the budget, log `PHASE_DEADLINE`, and resume from a persisted cursor next cycle. `PHASE_BUDGET_WARN` flags any phase using more than 40% of the budget (`SKFLEET_PHASE_BUDGET_WARN_SHARE`). On a wrapper timeout the niobe health record now carries `timeout_phase`, `last_phase_timings` and `phase_alerts` from the rotation actions.log, and keeps the last 16 KB of stderr after de-duplicating repeated lines with counts instead of the first 8 KB.
