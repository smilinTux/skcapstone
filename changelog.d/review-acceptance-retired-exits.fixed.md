Source review acceptance no longer reports already-finished pairs and exits of non-source-only reviews as `pending` every cycle. Finished pairs (bound `finished.json` + context digest, both native cards still done) are reported as historical acceptances without replaying the live launch policy, route snapshot or qualify recipe, which fail by construction after a policy or runtime change. Non-source-only review exits, which `collect()` refuses permanently, are classified `not-applicable`. Nothing new is accepted; the dispatcher logs these as one `SOURCE_REVIEW_ACCEPTANCE_RETIRED` count line.
