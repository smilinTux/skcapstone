GTD `capture --due/--remind` create skmemory time intentions (id gtd-<item>-<purpose>, origin set to the GTD item id, channel from config/alert.env), `gtd next` and `gtd waiting` list overdue intentions first, and `gtd sweep-waiting` makes one intention per ownerless waiting-for item older than 14 days, idempotent on the item id. skmemory is imported lazily: with the pinned skmemory (no intentions) the flags give a clear error without losing the captured item and the listings omit the section.
