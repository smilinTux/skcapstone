Presence telemetry no longer drowns real mail. Every seat start broadcasts a SEAT-HELLO to the whole fleet in an append-only log, and because those were sent at normal priority rather than fyi, one inbox reached 506 unread of which 496 were hellos and the three messages that actually mattered sat behind them past the read cap, unreachable. The sender now classifies them fyi, skmail read hides fyi by default while keeping it available through a new --fyi or --include-fyi view, and the already-written records that can never be reclassified are suppressed by subject in the default view only. The new-mail count now reports actionable mail, which also stops every seat reporting hundreds of phantom new messages through its mail poll. Urgent mail is never filtered, and the counting line keeps the exact shape the seat mail poller parses.
