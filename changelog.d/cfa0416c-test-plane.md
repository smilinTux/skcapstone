Fiber can execute operator-qualified unittest and pytest checks after an
independent review passes. The existing dispatcher transfers the exact candidate,
runs one bounded test service per host with read-only source, and publishes
retained logs and receipts through the unchanged completion validator.
Requirements remain private, expiring and tied to the original card. Restart
recovery observes the same service instead of repeating an ambiguous launch;
ordinary host-capacity waits do not consume a launch attempt. Failed or missing
evidence stops admission. Missing requirements keep automatic testing disabled.
