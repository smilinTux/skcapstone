The rotator pool fold is incremental: card folds and authoritative snapshots are cached by file signature and only changed cards are re-folded, with a per-cycle audit sample, and the card loop stops admitting cards at a soft deadline with a resume cursor. Review card reads no longer re-fold the whole board each time. This keeps the niobe cycle inside its dispatch timeout (it was timing out at 270 s on 2026-10-13).
