`gtd clarify / move / done / reopen` (CLI and MCP) and the dreaming engine's someday-maybe writer no longer rewrite the whole-file GTD lists on a host that is not the GTD owner (`coordination/gtd/OWNER`). They validate against the local copy, append a spool record through skos (`spool_mutation` / `spool_append`, smilinTux/skos#37) and answer `"queued": true, "owner": "<host>"` instead of claiming the list changed; the owner's `skos-gtd-drain.timer` applies them. On the owner, with no OWNER file, or without a skos that has the mutation API, behavior is unchanged.
