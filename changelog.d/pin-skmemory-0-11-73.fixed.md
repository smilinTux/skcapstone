The fleet pin moved to skmemory 0.11.73. It carries two fixes the chi hosts were missing: the reconcile prune now deletes the exact guard-approved orphan ids in one statement instead of sharing a public `flat_ids` keep table, which let concurrent per-agent syncs delete each other's rows (skmemory#81); and the AGE graph phase of `skmemory sync` is incremental and budgeted, while `sync` and `reindex` no longer write lossy preview stubs into the flat tier (skmemory#82).
