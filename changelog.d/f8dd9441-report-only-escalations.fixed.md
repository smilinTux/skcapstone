ATLAS has exactly two gates: the freeze, and one-time signed ratification (standard catalog actions) or auto-normal. Every escalation is now report-only: the loop writes no ITIL change and no ledger intent, only an acknowledge-only decision record. `skoperator decide --approve` refuses before any write and names `skoperator apps ratify <app> <action>`; `--reject` acknowledges, and `--authorization` is gone from `decide`. The dispatcher refuses, before the approval is spent, any intent whose approval would come from a human CAB vote; only standard and auto-normal approvals dispatch (`honor-pending --max-approval-age` is removed). `honor-pending` reports verified and report-only counts and exits 3 when an intent can never actuate. One decision record per episode; a new episode starts after the previous one was resolved and its condition read definitely cleared on the node that observed it. The Telegram card is report-only with a class-based hint (major or irreversible: handle by hand; reversible non-standard: ratify to make it auto-normal) and only an acknowledge command. Loop-drafted changes are authored by `operator`, so auto-normal changes auto-approve at the ITIL fold as designed. The CMDB apply gate and the trustee guard keep the signed human vote unchanged. Card f3add2c4 (node binding of approvals) is moot and not implemented; `observed_on` is informational only.
